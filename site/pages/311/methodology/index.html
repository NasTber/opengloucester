How the 311 numbers work

Everything comes from public requests on SeeClickFix, the city's service request system.

What's collected

  • Every public request since January 2024, checked daily.
  • For each one: category, location, and when it was submitted, acknowledged, closed, or reopened.
  • Not collected: descriptions, photos, or who submitted it.
  • Private requests and internal work orders aren't public, so they aren't counted. Requests later deleted or made private are removed.

Statuses

Open
Submitted, not yet acknowledged.
Acknowledged
City staff marked it as seen. Still counts as open. Only staff can do this.
Closed
Marked resolved. Anyone with an account can close a request, including the person who reported it.
Archived
Closed requests are archived after 7 days. Counts as closed.

Measures

Time to acknowledge
Submission to staff acknowledgment. Requests never acknowledged are left out. Since only staff can acknowledge, this is the most direct measure of the city's response.
Time to close
Submission to the latest close. If a request was archived without a close time, the archive time is used.
Typical (median)
Half took less time, half took more. Unlike an average, a few very old requests don't skew it.
1 in 10 took over
The 90th percentile: how long the slowest requests take.
Ward
Based on the request's map location and the 2022 MassGIS ward boundaries.
Per 1,000 residents
Requests divided by the ward's 2020 census population, times 1,000.

Keep in mind

  • A closed request isn't always a fixed problem. Duplicates and requests outside the city's responsibility also get closed.
  • Some departments use SeeClickFix more than others.
  • Ward differences reflect how often people report problems, not only how the city responds.
  • Recent months will change as requests close.
  • Numbers based on fewer than 5 requests aren't shown.
