About and methodology

{{ site.name }} is an independent, read-only site. It publishes public data about the City of {{ town.name }}, {{ town.state }}, and does not accept reports, comments, or accounts.

On this page

  • Principles
  • Data sources
  • Update schedule
  • AI-generated summaries
  • Corrections
  • Contact

Principles

  • Every number and every summary links to its source.
  • Text written by software is labeled as AI-generated.
  • The site reports figures without commentary.
  • Corrections are shown on the page, not made silently.
  • History is kept. When the city replaces or removes a document, the earlier version stays available here.

Data sources

Sources are listed here as each section goes live, with a link to the original data and its license.

  • 311 requests: public service requests from SeeClickFix. Data from seeclickfix.com, used under the Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 3.0 license. Private requests are not available and are not counted.
  • Meetings: the City of {{ town.name }} calendar and the agendas attached to each meeting, which the city stores in its Archive Center. Entries that are not meetings of a public body, such as community events, are left out. Each agenda is saved when it is first posted, and later versions are saved alongside it.
  • Ward boundaries: MassGIS, the Commonwealth of Massachusetts' geographic information office.

Metric definitions, including how each request status is counted, will be published here before the figures that use them.

Update schedule

An automated job collects new data once a day and rebuilds the site. The date at the bottom of every page shows the last update.

AI-generated summaries

Some plain-English summaries, such as meeting previews, are written by an AI language model from the original document. Each summary is labeled as AI-generated, links to the document it summarizes, and records when it was written. Summaries are not reviewed by a person before they are published. The original document is the authoritative source.

Corrections

If a figure or summary is wrong, please report it. Confirmed errors are fixed and a correction note is added to the affected page, stating what changed and when.
